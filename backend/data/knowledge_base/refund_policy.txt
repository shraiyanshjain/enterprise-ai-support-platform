REFUND POLICY

Customers can request a refund within 30 days of receiving their order.

The product must be unused and returned in its original packaging.

Refund requests for damaged products must include photographs of the damaged product.

Once the returned product is received and inspected, approved refunds are processed within 5-7 business days.

Digital products are not eligible for refunds after they have been downloaded.

For refund requests, customers should provide their order number and the reason for the refund.
